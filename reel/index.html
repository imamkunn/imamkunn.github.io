Script & shots

Story, dialogue and a shot list with a target length per shot, so generation only goes where the edit needs it.

Previz

A quick 3D blocking pass in Blender locks positions, camera side, eyelines and the 180° line.

Frames & video

Reference sheets, one continuity-checked first frame per shot (Nano Banana Pro), then image-to-video in Google Flow (Veo 3.1). Every clip is checked frame by frame and regenerated if anything breaks.

Sound & grade

ElevenLabs voices converted from the on-screen performance for lip-sync, generated foley and score, then a film grade (halation, grain, muted palette), 2.39 letterbox and −16 LUFS.
